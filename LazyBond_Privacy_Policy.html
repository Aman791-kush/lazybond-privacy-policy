LAZYBOND PRIVACY POLICY Effective Date: October 16, 2026 | Last Updated: October 16, 2026 Privacy Contact: supportlazybond@gmail.com 1. About LazyBond LazyBond is a social planning application that helps users create, discover, share, and join plans with friends and other users. This Privacy Policy explains how LazyBond collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when users access our mobile application and related services. 2. Information We Collect The information processed depends on the features you use, the information you provide, your device permissions, and the services integrated into the app. Account and Authentication Information We may process phone numbers used for OTP verification; information made available through Google Sign-In when you choose that method; user IDs, usernames, display names, and profile photographs; and authentication and account-related records. Profile and Plan Information When you use LazyBond, we may process profile details you submit; plan titles, descriptions, dates, and times; plan locations and participation details; information about plans you create, share, or join; and content and interactions submitted through available app features. Depending on the relevant feature and its visibility settings, information may be visible to friends, invited participants, or other users. Location Information LazyBond may access your device's location while you are using the app, if you grant location permission and use a feature that requires it. Location information may be used to support relevant location-based features, such as helping users discover nearby places or coordinate plans. LazyBond does not intentionally access device location in the background as part of the location functionality described in this policy. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location permission may limit features that depend on location. Messages and User-Submitted Content If you use messaging or other communication features, we may process message content and associated information, such as sender and recipient identifiers and timestamps, to deliver the feature and maintain service security.

Device and Technical Information Depending on the app configuration and integrated SDKs, information may include device type, operating system, and app version; app diagnostics and crash reports; technical logs and network information; and identifiers used for authentication, security, analytics, or app functionality. The exact information collected depends on the technologies enabled in the production version of LazyBond. 3. How We Use Information We use information, where applicable, to create and maintain user accounts; authenticate users and protect account security; enable users to create, discover, share, and join plans; provide profiles, messaging, search, and location-based functionality; send necessary service and security notifications; detect spam, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity; troubleshoot problems and improve reliability; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable legal obligations. We limit processing to purposes that are reasonably necessary for the service and permitted by applicable law. 4. Service Providers and Information Sharing LazyBond may use third-party service providers to support app functionality, including authentication, hosting, databases, search, diagnostics, and security. Depending on the services enabled in the current production release, these providers may include Google Firebase services and Typesense. Such providers may process relevant information to provide their services. The specific information processed depends on the integration and configuration. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the rights and security of LazyBond. If a business transfer occurs, information may be transferred as permitted by applicable law, with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. This statement is subject to our actual practices and applicable law. 5. Data Security We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. These safeguards may include access controls, secure authentication, HTTPS transmission, and protections provided by our infrastructure providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and report suspected unauthorized access. 6. Data Retention We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations. Retention periods vary depending on the information and its purpose. Content designed to expire or disappear may no longer be visible to users after the applicable period. This does not necessarily mean all backups or technical records are immediately erased. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or de-identified, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention obligations.

7. Account Deletion Users can initiate account deletion through the account deletion option available within the LazyBond app. Users who cannot access the app may also request deletion by emailing supportlazybond@gmail.com. Please use the subject line LazyBond Account Deletion Request and provide the account information reasonably necessary for us to identify and verify the request. Do not send passwords or OTP codes. We will process valid requests in accordance with applicable law and delete the account and associated personal information, except for information that we are legally permitted or required to retain. Where information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons, it will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and our retention practices. Account deactivation or suspension is not a substitute for account deletion. Google Play also requires a public external account-deletion resource URL to be entered in the designated Play Console field. Ensure this page is published and clearly explains how users can request deletion. An email-only request method should be verified against the current Play Console requirements before submission. 8. User Choices and Privacy Rights Depending on applicable law, users may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, or exercise other available privacy rights. Users can manage device permissions, including location access, through their device settings. For privacy requests or questions, contact supportlazybond@gmail.com. 9. Children's Privacy LazyBond is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law or the service's eligibility requirements. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action. 10. Third-Party Services LazyBond may rely on third-party software, infrastructure, and services. Their processing of information depends on the integrations enabled in our app and their applicable terms and privacy practices. We review relevant service-provider practices and seek to ensure that our use of these services complies with applicable requirements. 11. International Processing Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the user's country, depending on our infrastructure and service providers. Where applicable, we take appropriate measures to protect personal information in accordance with relevant data protection laws. 12. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, service providers, data practices, or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
